FAQs

How do I verify my email?

Check your inbox for the verification link. If it is missing, resend it from the Verify Email page.

I forgot my password. What should I do?

Use the “Forgot password” link on the login page to request a reset.

Why can’t I publish my content?

Only drafts can be published. Check that your content is in Draft status.

How do comments work?

Comments are available on published posts. If you cannot see them, refresh and try again.
