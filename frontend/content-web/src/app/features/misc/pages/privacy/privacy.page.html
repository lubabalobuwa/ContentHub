01 / OVERVIEW

Privacy is part of the product.

TechContentHub collects only the information required to operate a useful, secure publishing service. We do not sell your personal information.

This policy explains what we collect when you create an account, publish content, interact with articles, or contact support.

02 / WHAT WE COLLECT

The information we need

Account information

Your email address, display name, authentication details, and optional profile image.

Your content

Articles, drafts, uploaded images, comments, and other material you choose to provide.

Service data

Basic usage telemetry, security events, and error logs needed to operate the service reliably.

Support messages

The contact details and information you include when asking us for assistance.

03 / HOW WE USE IT

Purpose, not profiling

  • 01Provide, maintain, and secure ContentHub.
  • 02Publish and display content you create.
  • 03Improve reliability and performance.
  • 04Respond to questions and support requests.

04 / YOUR CHOICES

You stay in control.

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. You can also edit or remove your content through your account where those controls are available.

Our commitment

We do not sell your personal information or use it for third-party advertising.
